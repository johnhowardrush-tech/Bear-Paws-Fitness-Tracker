Built us a fitness tracker. Starts next month, let's go.

  https://johnhowardrush-tech.github.io/Bear-Paws-Fitness-Tracker


STEP 1 - SET UP YOUR PROFILE FIRST

Go to the "Profiles" tab, find YOUR name on the card, hit "Edit stats", and fill in:
  - Height in total inches (5'10" = 70)
  - Your starting weight
  - Goal weight (optional)
  - Starting blood pressure (optional)

Check the name on the card before you edit - they're in alphabetical order.
Your starting weight is your baseline, so get it right.


STEP 2 - CHECK IN EACH DAY

Go to "Check In":
  - Weight is REQUIRED every time
  - Blood pressure is optional (both numbers if you do it)
  - Did you work out? How long?
  - How many drinks?

Logged the wrong thing? Just pick that same date again and re-save. It updates
instead of duplicating.


HOW THE SCORING WORKS

Ranking is by PERCENT of body weight, not raw pounds - so we're even regardless
of size. 10 lbs off 180 is -5.56%, but 10 lbs off 220 is only -4.55%.

Green and negative = going down. Red and positive = going up.

Blood pressure gets scored with MAP (one number from your two), and the BP
leaderboard ranks by how many points you've dropped from your own baseline.
So it rewards improvement, not good genes.


BETS

Everyone starts at 100 points. Bet on whether someone hits a goal - weight or
blood pressure. Someone else takes the other side, winner takes the pot.

No real money. Just bragging rights.


Heads up: no login, we all see everyone's numbers. Don't put anything in there
you wouldn't say out loud.
